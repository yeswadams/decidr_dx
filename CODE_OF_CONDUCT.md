# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Decidr a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, respectful, constructive, and open to differing viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed in the repository hosting service. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
